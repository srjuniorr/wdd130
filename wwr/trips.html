Our Rafting Adventures

From the gentle curves of the Emerald Waters to the heart-pounding drops of Dragon's Breath, we offer an adventure for every soul. Choose your journey below.

Crystal Cascade

Perfect for beginners and families, the Crystal Cascade trip offers a delightful mix of gentle rapids and stunning scenery. Enjoy the crystal clear waters and lush green canyons as you drift through the heart of nature.

Highlights: Scenic photography, swimming holes, and local wildlife sightings.

Dragon's Breath

Seek the thrill? Dragon's Breath is our most intense expedition. Navigate through narrow canyons and Class V rapids that will test your spirit. This trip is designed for experienced paddlers looking for the ultimate adrenaline rush.

Highlights: 15-foot drops, the "Dragon's Throat" chute, and expert-level maneuvering.

Mystic River Sunset

Experience the magic of the river as the sun sets. Our Mystic River evening trip combines moderate Class II rapids with the tranquil beauty of the golden hour. It's the perfect way to unwind and witness the river in a different light.

Highlights: Gourmet evening snack, sunset perspectives, and starry-night return.
